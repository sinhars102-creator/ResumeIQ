Senior Brand Manager - Lumora Living (consumer lifestyle and beauty brand), Hyderabad / Bengaluru hybrid

About the role
Lumora Living is a fast-growing consumer brand. We are looking for a Senior Brand Manager to own brand positioning and build integrated campaigns that drive both brand love and business growth.

What you will do
- Own brand positioning and the brand architecture across categories; keep the brand voice consistent across every touchpoint
- Plan and lead integrated campaigns across digital, social, influencer, OOH and retail, from creative brief to measurement
- Manage creative, media and influencer agencies; own the annual brand and campaign budget and report ROI to leadership
- Turn consumer insights and segmentation research into campaign ideas and product communication
- Partner with product, performance marketing and sales to link brand activity to revenue, acquisition cost and retention
- Mentor junior brand executives and set the measurement framework for brand health (awareness, consideration, engagement)

What we are looking for
- 4 to 7 years in brand or integrated marketing at a consumer, D2C, lifestyle or beauty brand
- Proven record of campaigns with measurable outcomes (reach, engagement, sales lift, CPA)
- Strong agency and budget management experience
- Strong consumer research, analytics and storytelling skills (Google Analytics, Meta Ads Manager)
- Experience with retail or trade marketing, and with e-commerce marketplaces, is a plus
- MBA in marketing preferred
