Director of Finance - Corporate Finance, Western India (Mumbai)
Company: Aldervale Global Services (a listed multinational financial-services group)

About the role
Aldervale is looking for a Director of Finance to lead financial strategy, planning and performance for its India business, partnering with the Managing Director and the regional CFO.

Responsibilities
- Own the annual budget, rolling forecast and long-range plan; lead variance analysis and drive accountability with business heads
- Act as the finance business partner to the leadership team on pricing, investments and P&L performance
- Evaluate capital allocation and investment proposals; build and review financial models and valuations
- Support M&A, due diligence and integration activity in partnership with Corporate Development
- Own financial governance: internal controls, statutory and audit readiness, policy compliance and risk management
- Prepare and present board, audit-committee and investor-ready reporting; communicate clearly with senior executives
- Lead, coach and develop a finance team of 10-15 across FP&A, controllership and business finance
- Drive finance transformation: automation, ERP and BI enablement, process improvement

Requirements
- 10+ years in corporate finance, FP&A or business finance, with at least 3 years in a senior leadership role
- CA / CFA / MBA Finance preferred
- Strong financial modelling, valuation and capital-allocation experience
- Experience with IFRS / Ind AS reporting and treasury or tax exposure is a plus
- Hands-on with SAP or Oracle, Power BI or similar; advanced Excel
- Proven executive communication and stakeholder management
