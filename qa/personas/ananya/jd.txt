Group Product Manager, Growth Platform - Orbita Cloud (Series D, Bengaluru / hybrid)

About the role
Orbita Cloud is a fast-growing B2B SaaS company building a revenue operations platform used by 4,000+ companies. We are looking for a Group Product Manager to own our Growth Platform area (onboarding, activation, pricing and packaging, and lifecycle) and lead a team of product managers.

What you will do
- Set the product strategy and multi-quarter roadmap for the Growth Platform area and tie it to company revenue and retention goals
- Lead, coach and grow a team of 3-4 product managers; set the bar for product quality and discovery
- Partner with engineering, design, data science, sales and marketing leaders to prioritise and deliver
- Run a rigorous experimentation program across activation, conversion and monetisation
- Own pricing and packaging decisions end to end, working with finance and sales
- Define and track success metrics (activation, retention, expansion revenue, NRR) and be accountable for business outcomes
- Communicate strategy and trade-offs to executives and stakeholders

What you bring
- 7+ years in product management, with at least 2 years leading or mentoring product managers
- Strong track record of shipping B2B SaaS products with measurable impact on retention, activation or revenue
- Deep comfort with data: SQL, product analytics, experiment design
- Experience with pricing, packaging and monetisation
- Excellent stakeholder management and communication
- Nice to have: experience with PLG motions, machine learning products, or international expansion
