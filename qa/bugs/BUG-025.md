## Steps to reproduce
1. Laptop: ask 3 questions in the workspace.  2. iPhone SE: Review N.

## Expected
The suggestion card keeps most of the right column; the sheet shows the whole Current/Suggested pair or scrolls it clearly.

Laptop: after 2-3 turns the card list is about 250 px tall (the Apply buttons of the top card are off screen, e.g. A2, B2). iPhone SE: the sheet covers about 60% of the screen; the diff shows two lines ('high-scale high-scale payments and ...') and the Apply bar; the user must scroll inside a small window to read the change.

- Screenshots: qa/runs/2026-11-25_1425/screens/laptop-chrome/A2-ESC003-correction.png, B2-weakest-bullets.png; qa/runs/2026-11-25_1425/screens/iphone-se/S3-sheet-first-card.png

## Probable cause
src/ResumeLLM.css:413-414 (.ws-dock, .ws-thread max-height: 34vh); .ws-sheet fixed height on phones.

## Suggested fix
Cap the thread at about 25% of the column (collapsed older turns); let the sheet grow to 75% when the diff is long.
