Refund and cancellation policy

Last updated 26 November 2026 · Applies to payments made on the ResumeIQ website (www.myresumeiq.io) · Operated by Rahul Sinha, India

In short: editing and previewing your resume is free. You pay only to download the PDF. If you pay and the PDF is not delivered, we refund you in full.

What you pay for

  • 1 download: ₹79, one PDF, no expiry.
  • 10 downloads: ₹399, up to 10 PDFs within 30 days of payment.
  • Unlimited: ₹799, as many PDFs as you need within 30 days of payment.

These are one-time payments, made through Razorpay by UPI, card or netbanking. Nothing renews automatically and there is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. A 10-download or unlimited plan simply ends after 30 days.

When you get a full refund

  • You paid, but the download did not become available to you (for example the plan was not activated on your account).
  • You paid, but the PDF could not be created or saved after several attempts, so you never received your resume.
  • You were charged more than once for the same plan by mistake.

When we cannot refund

Once your PDF has been created and downloaded successfully, the digital product has been delivered, so we do not refund a change of mind, or a plan you did not use up. If the content of the PDF has a mistake that comes from ResumeIQ and not from the text you approved, write to us and we will fix it or refund you.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email rahul@myresumeiq.io from the email address you signed in with.
  2. Tell us what happened, and include the payment ID or the date, time and amount of the payment. A screenshot of any error helps.
  3. We reply within 2 working days. If your request qualifies, we start the refund within 5 working days, to the same UPI account, card or bank account you paid from. After we start it, your bank or card network normally takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.

Your rights

Nothing here limits the rights you have under Indian consumer law.
